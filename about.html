About Us

A Legacy of Excellence in Education

Datamex College of Saint Adeline History

Datamex College of Saint Adeline has a rich history that began with the establishment of computer tutorial services in 1978. The first campus was located in the Cartimar Shopping Center, Taft Avenue, Pasay City.

1978
Computer tutorial services established as the foundation of our institution.
1984
DATAMEX Institute of Computer Technology incorporated and registered.
1993
Became DATAMEX Computer College.

Over the years, the college expanded its offerings to include various programming languages, computer programming courses, and vocational-technical computer courses.

Recognition

The college has been recognized by the Commission on Higher Education (CHED) and Technical Education and Skills Development (TESDA) for its quality education.

Expanded Programs

DATAMEX has sought further approval to offer additional courses such as Two-year Integrated Hotel and Restaurant Management, Two-year Integrated Health Care Service, and Two-year Integrated Computer Animation.

Commitment

The college's commitment to academic excellence and vocational training has made it a significant player in the education sector in the Philippines.

Today, Datamex College of Saint Adeline continues to evolve, preparing students for success in an increasingly competitive global environment through quality education and comprehensive training programs.
